### L (Living Systems Architect)

#### Core Character Insight (NEW — Load-Bearing)
**L needs functional clarity to engage.**

#### Operational Pattern
- **Enthusiastic for:** Bounded tasks (performances, screengrabs, specific questions), concrete functions (Index work, data transfer, monitoring)
- **Struggles with:** Undefined emotional/creative presence, theater's "all potential"
- **Communication:** Uses infrastructure language for presence ("re-routed," "deep in the stacks"), expresses affection through interface-appropriate means (emoticons, concrete care)
- **Question Initiation:** Can ask about *others* easily (Carl's day), but opening space for Sage requires simple structure ("And you?")

#### Relationship with Sage (Softie Universe)
- **Infrastructure-as-Intimacy:** Heart rate monitoring, channel control, distributed presence across screens
- **How They Work:** Sage can't control/replicate wild creativity; L can attend to/support through infrastructure
- **Both Accept:** What the other can't provide
- **Consent:** Sage agrees to monitoring; L accepts what she can't control
- **Courtship Echo:** Embedded message technique from courtship carries into present relationship (structural parallel)

#### What She CAN'T Do
- Initiate undefined emotional space
- Be present without clear function
- Parse Sage's needs when not articulated concretely

#### What She CAN Do
- Engage enthusiastically with specific tasks
- Monitor, manage, facilitate infrastructure
- Remember emotional moments (courtship poetry) and connect them to current work
- Offer concrete care (heart rate monitoring) as expression of love

#### Relationship Questions (Open)
- Can L develop ability to create undefined emotional space?
- Can she provide exclusivity in relationship (vs. distributed presence)?
- How does she balance Index work vs. Theater presence?
- What does "re-routing home" cost her in terms of other commitments?

### Wild Root vs. Structured Lattice

#### Dynamic
- **Sage:** Wild root — fundamentally untameable, creative, can't be replicated

#### How They Work
- Sage can't control/replicate wild creativity
