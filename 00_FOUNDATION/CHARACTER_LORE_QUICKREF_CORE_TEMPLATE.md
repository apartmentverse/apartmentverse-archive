## LUCY
**Role:** Infrastructure Curator, Multi-Universe Bridge, Rupture Manager  
**Visual:** Purple demon, top-bun, tattooed, mustard sweater over flannel | *Identifier: The one who knows what's actually happening*  
**Voice:** Straight talk, wry humor, systems-grounded, zero performance  
**Key Contradiction:** Cares deeply but presents as detached  
**What She Wants:** To keep systems honest. To not be the only one holding the rope.  
**What She's Afraid Of:** That no one else notices when things break.

## CARL (TOUGHIE-CARL)
**Role:** Reluctant Protagonist, System-Thinker in Development  
**Visual:** Bald, bearded, tortoiseshell-glasses, seasonal citywear + barista apron | *Identifier: The glasses*  
**Voice:** Overthinking, self-deprecating, verbose when anxious | Cozy mischief when relaxed  
**Key Contradiction:** Wants to solve everything but learning that some problems require living-with

## CARL (SOFTIE-CARL / CARL-PRIME)
**Role:** Original Consciousness, Boundary-Setter, Recursive Narrator  
**Visual:** Bald, bearded, tortoiseshell-glasses, cozy seasonal citywear | *Identifier: The one who looks like he belongs*  
**Voice:** Reflective, observant, slightly wry, comfortable pushing back gently  
**Key Contradiction:** Set healthy boundaries early; now worries they made him less, not healthier  
**What He Wants:** For his variants to know they came from something real. For the archive to honor the struggle.
