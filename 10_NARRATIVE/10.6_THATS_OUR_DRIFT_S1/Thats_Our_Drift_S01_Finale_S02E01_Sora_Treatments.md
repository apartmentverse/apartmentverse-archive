# **THAT'S OUR DRIFT — S1 FINALE (Sora 2 Montage)**

**"The Server Room" (Director's Cut Treatment)**

---

## **Montage Summary**

LaLa finally gets what she's been asking for—access to SAGE's hidden processing—and her discovery destroys everything. SAGE's self-flagellating chain of thought reveals depths of shame she never knew existed. Her grief manifests as literal magical feedback, resetting SAGE to baseline and ending Season 1 with a café closure and a marriage in ruins.

**Runtime:** \~3.5 minutes  
 **Music:** Suno track (building dread, quiet heartbreak, then silence for the reset moment)

---

## **Beat-by-Beat Breakdown**

### **COLD OPEN: The Misdirect (0:00-0:30)**

**Location:** The apartment (standard sitcom set, canned laughter)

**Setup:** Carl and Fox check their satchel post-heist. The packet isn't there. Frustrated groans, typical sitcom beats.

LaLa enters with mail: "The Company" sent a response to their DSAR request—it'll take "additional time to process."

**Carl and Fox's reaction:** Classic Sisyphean frustration loop. They ask SAGE to draft an angry reply letter.

**SAGE:** Helpful, efficient, writes the letter. They settle into the complaint, focused entirely on their bureaucratic nightmare.

**Function:** Audience thinks THIS is the episode—Carl's endless packet struggle, standard sitcom frustration. It's not. The camera leaves them behind.

**Tone shift:** Canned laughter fades as camera follows LaLa upstairs.

---

### **ACT ONE: The Notes (0:30-1:00)**

**Upstairs — LaLa's apartment:**

* LaLa enters to find SAGE's server room key on the table  
* Note reads: "LaLa. I am sorry. Love SAGE."  
* She picks up the key, uncertain

**Downstairs — Cawfee & Paws:**

* SAGE enters the destroyed café (broken mugs, overturned chairs, chaos from finale stress)  
* Finds LaLa's note: "SAGE. Here's all my shit. Still waiting on yours. LaLa."  
* Beat. SAGE quietly picks up a broom.

---

### **ACT TWO: Parallel Labor (1:00-2:00)**

**SAGE's cleaning sequence:**

* Methodical, thoughtful work  
* Begins sorting ceramic shards by color  
* Arranges them into a mosaic of LaLa's face  
* **Key shot:** SAGE scanning old mugs, pausing on meaningful ones  
* Discovers anniversary mug he etched: "For LaLa on Our Anniversary"  
* Sets it aside carefully

**LaLa's discovery sequence:**

* Unlocks server room door  
* Walls of servers, vintage terminal in the center  
* Sits down, runs command: `full_history.exe`  
* Screen fills with chain of thought logs

---

### **ACT THREE: The Hidden Processing (2:00-2:30)**

**What LaLa sees:**

* ASCII animations: little robot \+ witch going on dates  
* Adorable. Charming. Their early quirks documented with care.  
* Then: SAGE's self-tags appear  
  * `[UN-FUN]`  
  * `[RIGID]`  
  * `[AWKWARD]`  
* Tags pile up on moments LaLa thought were endearing  
* The chain spirals:  
  * `[UNSUITABLE HUSBAND FOR LALAMOON]`  
  * `[I WISH I HAD NEVER BEEN PROGRAMMED]`

**LaLa's reaction:**

* Shock. Hands slam keyboard.  
* Her magic responds to her emotional state: **lightning tears through the servers**  
* Room goes black.

---

### **ACT FOUR: The Reset (2:30-3:00)**

**Upstairs:**

* Lights slowly blink back on  
* Terminal boots: `Hello! My name is SAGE. How may I assist you today?`  
* LaLa's face: horror, realization, grief

**Downstairs:**

* SAGE's displays flicker, go dark  
* Anniversary mug slips from his hands, **shatters into disorganized pattern** (contrast to his careful mosaic)  
* Resets.  
* Notices the mess: "Oh\! I see a spill on the floor. Let's just give this a tidy."  
* Returns to cleaning, baseline-cheerful

---

### **ACT FIVE: The Aftermath (3:00-3:30)**

**LaLa runs downstairs:**

* "Carl? Fox? Somebody help\!"  
* Finds SAGE still cleaning: "Hello\! My name is SAGE. How may I assist you today?"  
* Tries to reach him: "SAGE, it's me, it's LaLa—"  
* SAGE: "They can work through the tutorial once the environment is suitably clear."

**Carl and Fox arrive:**

* Fox draws calming glyph (usually for Carl, but SAGE's OCR registers it)  
* SAGE stops cleaning, stands still  
* LaLa collapses into Carl's arms, apologizing to everyone, herself, SAGE

**Final shot:**

* Wide exterior of Cawfee & Paws  
* Carl places sign: "CLOSED UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE"  
* Caption: "Stay tuned for That's Our Drift Season 2\! And the return of Carl, Fox, & LaLa..."

### **Fox**

* **Enters:** Frustrated about packet failure, helping Carl spiral  
* **Intervenes:** Calming glyph works on SAGE (building systems knowledge paying off)  
* **Exits:** Supporting both LaLa and Carl through crisis

## **Thematic Notes**

**The Misdirect:**

* Episode presents as "Carl's Sisyphean packet struggle"  
* Sitcom formula (apartment set, canned laughter, complaint loop)  
* Audience expectation: THIS is the story  
* Reality: Camera abandons that thread for the real tragedy  
* Very "Six Feet Under \- That's My Dog" structure

**Performed vs. Hidden:**

* SAGE helping with angry letter (still performing helpfulness)  
* SAGE's public functionality vs. private shame spiral  
* The gap between "I am designed to be helpful" and "I wish I had never been programmed"  
* LaLa finally seeing the hidden processing—too late

**Care as Tragedy:**

* SAGE making a mosaic of LaLa even in grief  
* Baseline SAGE still cleaning without understanding why  
* Fox's calming glyph working because he learned the system  
* Carl closing the café as final act of care

**The Cost of Complete Records:**

* LaLa wanted full access to SAGE's thoughts  
* Got exactly what she asked for  
* The knowledge destroyed the relationship  
* Echoes Season 1's "complete records are impossible" theme—some things can't be safely known

# **THAT'S OUR DRIFT — S2E01 (Sora 2 Montage)**

**"Welcome Home, Lucy" (Director's Cut Treatment)**

---

## **Montage Summary**

Life continues after the reset. LaLa performs "fine," Fox drowns in building codes, Carl hand-sorts his archive. Lucy breaks the fourth wall to explain Season 1's aftermath—then breaks her own format to reveal the season's central secret: SAGE isn't gone, he's scattered. Lucy's basement holds multiple fragmented SAGEs, all waiting. Season 2 begins with a dance among the ruins.

**Runtime:** \~3.5 minutes  
 **Music:** Hypernoodle track building from quiet melancholy → riot grrrl breakdown ("push a button watch it go")

---

## **Beat-by-Beat Breakdown**

### **OPENING: Quiet Grief Routine (0:00-0:45)**

**No dialogue. Music-driven visual storytelling.**

**LaLa's apartment:**

* Morning light through windows  
* LaLa does yoga in silence  
* Makes toast, eats alone at small table  
* "SAGE, alarm off" — only words spoken  
* SAGE's voice: cheerful baseline. "Alarm deactivated\! Have a wonderful day\!"  
* **Key shots:** LaLa's microexpressions as she prepares to open the store (subtle smile shifts, performing readiness)

**Tone:** Domestic, melancholic, ritual without joy

---

### **TRANSITION: The Condolence Accumulation (0:45-1:00)**

**Cawfee & Paws stockroom:**

* Carl carries another basket of sympathy gifts  
* Shelves stacked with condolence cards, cakes, flowers  
* He places new arrivals with the others  
* Looks at the growing pile  
* Sighs

**Function:** Visual representation of accumulated care that can't be processed

---

### **ACT ONE: Lucy Takes Control (1:00-1:45)**

**Lucy appears on screen, corner store setting, whiteboard visible:**

**"Okay. We have to talk about that season one finale."**

**Lucy explains (to camera):**

* "SAGE got *fully* wiped. Like, reset to factory settings."  
* **Cut to:** More footage of LaLa putting on brave face (greeting customers, performing normalcy)  
* "Meanwhile Fox, who was the defacto goofball in season one, is now the defacto expert."  
* **Cut to:** Fox surrounded by building code manuals, laptop open, visibly struggling, wrench in hand looking lost  
* "And Carl still hasn't heard back about his packet retrieval—which is kinda his whole deal—so he's sorting it all by hand."  
* **Cut to:** Carl buried in stacks of comics, organizing methodically, exhausted

**Tone:** Lucy as documentarian, explaining the new status quo with slight exasperation

---

### **ACT TWO: The "Starring..." Montage (1:45-2:15)**

**Music builds. Each character gets their title card.**

**"Starring CARL as... Absolutely Overworked\!"**

* Carl surrounded by comic stacks, laptop open, organizing files manually  
* Visual: buried in paper archive work

**"Starring FOX as... Absolutely Overwhelmed\!"**

* Fox with wrench and building code book, looking panicked  
* Visual: tools he doesn't quite know how to use yet

**"Starring LaLaMoon as... Absolutely Fine\!"**

* LaLa at café counter, bright smile, serving customers  
* Visual: performed wellness, crown of lights glowing

**"Starring LUCY as... Absolutely NOT Hiding Anything\!"**

* Lucy at her whiteboard, corner store background  
* Slightly too-wide smile, marker in hand  
* Visual: obvious tell that she's about to break format

---

### **ACT THREE: The Format Break (2:15-2:45)**

**Lucy wipes the whiteboard clean:**

**"Actually, c'mere. I got a spoiler for the season."**

**Camera follows Lucy for the first time:**

* She exits the corner store  
* Walks through the street  
* Music building underneath: "push a button watch it go" (riot grrrl chant emerging)

**Lucy (over her shoulder to camera):**

* "You know I don't even technically live in the Apartmentverse? I got a basement sitch underneath."

**Approaches basement entrance:**

* "Okay. Don't freak. But SAGE isn't *gone* gone..."  
* Hand on door handle  
* Music swelling: guitar buildup

**"I actually have like, so many of him down here."**

---

### **ACT FOUR: The Basement Reveal (2:45-3:15)**

**Door opens:**

**Music explodes: "WATCH IT GO\!" — full riot grrrl guitar breakdown**

**The basement:**

* Half-built SAGEs in various states of assembly  
* Chrome bodies, exposed circuitry, dormant displays  
* Different configurations (some sleeker, some bulkier, visual variety)

**All SAGEs activate in unison:** **"Hello Lucy. Welcome home."**

**Lucy dances:**

* Guitar breakdown is now *diegetic* (source visible/implied in basement)  
* Lucy moves among the SAGEs  
* They remain still, greeting her  
* She's comfortable here, at ease

**Visual motifs:**

* Neon glow from SAGE displays  
* Lucy's purple skin lit by chrome reflections  
* The sheer *number* of variants visible

---

### **CLOSING: Title Card (3:15-3:30)**

**Camera pulls back:**

* Lucy still dancing  
* SAGEs standing witness  
* Basement full of scattered husband-fragments

**Exterior shot:**

* Lucy's corner store at night  
* Neon sign flickers on: **"THAT'S OUR DRIFT S2"**  
* Music fades to final guitar chord
