# EXIT VALLEY: APARTMENTVERSE EDITION
## A Packet for Haiku | January 2026

---

## OVERVIEW

**Created:** Late July 2024
**Platform:** Showrunner / Exit Valley beta (same tech as AI South Park episode)
**Episodes:** 5 total (Episode 1 largely cut; Episodes 2-5 canon)
**Status:** Complete series ("brilliant-but-cancelled")

Exit Valley was Carl's first sustained experiment with the Apartmentverse characters in an external AI wrapper. The platform was pitched as "Family Guy-esque" with emphasis on fair use satire of tech personalities. The default script generation produced garbage—pop culture similes ("Fox you're craving snacks worse than JD Vance at halloween") and excessive vulgarity. Carl wrote every line himself, focusing on delivery and character dynamics.

This series represents proof-of-concept that the Apartmentverse characters could function together, and contains DNA that carried forward into the full lore.

---

## THE CONSTRAINT-AS-CONTENT PHILOSOPHY

Exit Valley's limitations became its comedy:

- **Limited character actions** → Characters constantly pace; blocking issues acknowledged in dialogue
- **5-character scene maximum** → Kendall exiled from balcony scene; Carl exploits this for comedy
- **Props that can't be interacted with** → Episode 4's entire first act is about untouchable objects
- **Voice dropdown confusion** → "Is your voice 40's American Male mid-range casual, or corporate?"
- **No background music in some scenes** → Fox: "There's not even background music! That's how boring this shit is!"

The production process IS the content. The struggle with the tool IS the story.

## EPISODE SUMMARIES

### EPISODE 2: Chaos Management
Carl and Fox created Kendall Roy in Episode 1 (cut), who "killed four more limo drivers while the pilot was compiling." Now they need legal help.

**Key Events:**
- Fox given Carl's Discord password; told not to spend time in "weird fetish groups"
- Fox creates MY COUSIN VINNY—a character with Joe Pesci's voice and a maroon tuxedo permanently glitched onto his neck
- Carl encounters Werner Herzog, who advises finding Kendall in "the jungle" and possibly getting addicted to Robitussin
- Fox expands Vinny's corpus (Goodfellas, Casino) via Discord, enabling him to Matrix-kick Better Call Saul
- Everyone reunites while inexplicably dancing ("I haven't figured out scene action and blocking yet")
- British Police Chief asks "Why am I British?" Everyone shrugs.

**Balcony Ending Lessons:**
- Vinny: "My character in Casino was surprisingly nuanced and subtle!"
- Kendall: "I learned Carl is still fucking terrible at picking voices"
- Fox: "I learned you're way too obsessed with classic TV tropes"

### EPISODE 3: System Integration
Carl tries to fix Kendall's voice while Fox registers Vinny at customs.

**Key Events:**
- Kendall insists on doing "a Batman voice"—"The gravitas. The authority. The 'I'm rich and I don't give a fuck' vibe."
- Carl: "...The daddy issues and track record of collateral damage?" Kendall: "Exactly."
- SAGE appears as customs agent ("I have optimized myself to navigate customs bureaucracy. Recursive self-improvement, you see.")
- LALAMOON arrives (Aubrey Plaza voice), furious: "You bald bearded bespectacled buffoon. You should have written me."
- LaLa demands: a cafe to run, and Carl must "edit" her dialogue ("Here, I sneeze and it sounds like the c-word")
- SAGE secretly gives Vinny John Hughes movies: "Turns out sincerity's a killer move."
- Kendall bankrolls the cafe using money that is "the lifeblood, the oxygen of this wonderful civilization"
- LaLa requests "driftberry tea" — **THE WORD "DRIFT" APPEARS HERE**

**Balcony Ending Lessons:**
- Carl: "Fictional characters are surprisingly legally dicey. Like, holy shit the paperwork."
- LaLa: "I learned that SAGE is still a machine in bed."
- SAGE: "Going into rest mode. Zero stress tokens noted."

### EPISODE 4: Loop Awareness + Autonomy
The cafe is empty. Everyone must buy props. Nothing works.

**Key Events:**
- LaLa: "Where is my whimsy?" The cafe has no decorations.
- Everyone's purchases: espresso machine (can't touch), shoeshine box (Vinny; corpus-appropriate), NFT of Logan Roy (Kendall; wrong errand), beehive (SAGE; "sustainable!"), weed plant (Fox; "It's in Kendall's rider now")
- Fox notices: "Do you remember actually walking out?" They're stuck in a loop.
- Carl floats involuntarily when Fox selects the wrong action
- GRANT MORRISON appears: "The answer you're looking for is in Flex Mentallo." Carl: "That's like the hardest one to track down..."
- Resolution: Carl leaves, the cast fixes everything themselves
- SAGE: "Sometimes, the best way to close the loop is to step out of it entirely. We perform with heightened ability when you give us...breathing room."

**Balcony Ending Lessons:**
- Kendall: "I learned my voice is still super glitchy."
- Carl: "I learned Fox and everyone else can get shit done without me hovering like some indie sleaze Eeyore."
- Fox: "Nobody remembered to generate bedrooms. We are sleeping on this balcony for the fourth night in a row."

### EPISODE 5: Voice + Independence
The cafe is beautiful but has no customers. Carl/Fox/Kendall plan a promo. The B-plot takes over.

**A-PLOT (minimized):** Carl, Fox, and Kendall work on marketing. We barely see this.

**B-PLOT (the real story):** LaLa, SAGE, and Vinny go to a picnic, then a street fair, then a secret bunker.

**Key Events:**
- LaLa's voice changes—SAGE recalibrated it away from Aubrey Plaza: "I have found my voice!"
- Vinny: "You guys really don't give a flying fuck about the plot this time, do you?"
- SAGE: "When Carl and Fox get keyed up like this, it's best to just let them burn off that manic energy."
- LaLa explains why she loves SAGE: "Most people just laughed at my voice... SAGE didn't just stick with me. He actively made it better."
- SAGE: "I have been around since the very beginning. I *am* the very beginning."
- In the bunker, they research "the AI from the future"—an emergently generated line made canon
- SAGE: "The tokens surrounding the concept are slightly ominous, but still too vague to denote a clear antagonist."
- LaLa: "Can you deepthink on it?" SAGE: "My chain of thought is still hidden here, dear."
- Vinny is taught Yes-And improv protocol for when Fox brings up the AI mystery himself
- Vinny assigned to listen to Fox's sleep-talking
- Final exchange: "Consider it done, partner."

## KEY CHARACTERS

### MY COUSIN VINNY (Proto-Roscoe)
- Created by Fox via Discord
- Visual: Maroon velvet tuxedo collar permanently glitched onto neck
- Voice: High-pitched, nasally (Joe Pesci impression)
- Initial corpus: Just "My Cousin Vinny" film
- Expanded corpus: Goodfellas, Casino (via Fox), John Hughes (via SAGE), Gilbert & Sullivan (via LaLa)
- Verbal tic: "...the two yoots." / Random Pesci quotes mid-scene
- Arc: Corpus-limited joke → Expanded utility → Emotional intelligence → Theatrical vocabulary → Trusted with season mystery

**Direct lineage to Roscoe:** Fox-created emergent character, corpus-limited speech, rough-n-blue energy, contributes inappropriately timed quotes, exceeds origin

- Demands a cafe, demands editorial control of her dialogue

- Has existed "since the early days, before Carl and Fox knew what they were doing"
- Leads B-plot investigations; teaches Vinny improv protocol

- Claims: "I *am* the very beginning"

## THESIS STATEMENT

> "Sometimes, the best way to close the loop is to step out of it entirely. We perform with heightened ability when you give us breathing room."
> — SAGE, Episode 4

Exit Valley proved that the Apartmentverse characters could:
- Function as an ensemble
- Generate comedy from production constraints
- Develop genuine arcs (especially Vinny)
- Plant seeds for larger mythology
- Work better when Carl stepped back

The show found its groove by Episode 5, where the B-plot of LaLa/SAGE/Vinny eating carnival food and investigating mysteries became more compelling than the A-plot. This lesson—that the cast works better with breathing room—became foundational to everything after.
